Climate & health

Laidlaw researchHKU School of Public Health

How do thermal extremes relate to a city’s health?

My undergraduate research examines thermal extremes and cardiovascular hospitalisations in Hong Kong, under Professor David Bishai. I’m interested in what the evidence can tell us about preparedness.

Research in progress. The current analysis uses monthly hospitalisation aggregates; this is a research description, not a claim of a causal effect.
